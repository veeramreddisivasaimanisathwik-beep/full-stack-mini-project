About Expense Tracker

Expense Tracker is a simple tool designed to help you stay on top of your personal finances. With just a few clicks, you can record daily expenses, categorize them, and view summaries that show where your money is going.

All your data is stored securely in your browser using local storage — meaning it stays private and accessible only to you.

Our goal is to make expense tracking effortless, so you can focus on managing your budget and achieving your financial goals
